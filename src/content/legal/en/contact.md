## Operator

LUDWISE is operated by Daniel Kindl, a private individual in the Czech Republic.

## General contact

Use [hello@ludwise.com](mailto:hello@ludwise.com) for support, corrections, legal notices, data-rights requests, press, and other private matters.

LUDWISE is operated by one person and promises no answer time.
If a matter remains open and you receive no answer, you can send a follow-up.

## Security reports

Use [security@ludwise.com](mailto:security@ludwise.com) for a vulnerability or other security-sensitive report.
See the public [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/ludwise/ludwise-web/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for reporting guidance.

## Public issue tracker

For a public software bug or a wrong price, you can also use the [LUDWISE web issue tracker](https://github.com/ludwise/ludwise-web/issues).
Do not put personal, legal, account, security, or other private information in a public issue.
Use email for those matters.
