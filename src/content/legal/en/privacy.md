## Who operates LUDWISE

LUDWISE is operated by Daniel Kindl, a private individual in the Czech Republic.
Daniel Kindl is the controller for the personal data described on this page.

You can contact the controller at [hello@ludwise.com](mailto:hello@ludwise.com).
See [Contact](/legal/contact) for the other public contact routes.

## Website requests and operational logs

Cloudflare delivers the LUDWISE website. Cloudflare receives network data that is needed to deliver a request.
This data can include an IP address, connection data, TLS data, and request metadata.

The LUDWISE application does not put an IP address in its structured request log.
It also excludes full URLs, query strings, cookies, authorization headers, and user-agent values.
The application log can contain the request method, route template, response status, duration, and Cloudflare Ray ID.

LUDWISE uses these records to operate the service, find faults, and protect the service.
The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure service.
Operational records are kept only while they are needed for these purposes.
Cloudflare account settings control the exact platform-log retention period.

## Theme preference

LUDWISE uses one first-party cookie named `theme` after you use the theme control.
The cookie stores only `light` or `dark`.
It lets the server render your selected theme on the next page.
The cookie expires after one year.

LUDWISE does not use `localStorage` or `sessionStorage` for the MVP.
The theme cookie is not used for advertising, analytics, or visitor profiling.
See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) for more detail.

## Analytics

LUDWISE runs no product analytics at launch.
It sends no page-view event and no analytics event to LUDWISE or to an analytics provider.
There is no analytics provider, advertising tracker, or external tracking script in the public site.

## Contact email

Fastmail hosts mail for `ludwise.com` and acts as the mail processor.
The operator reads messages sent to the public LUDWISE addresses.
No other LUDWISE staff member reads them because there is no other staff member.

For ordinary support and project mail, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
The legitimate interest is to receive and answer messages about LUDWISE.
For a data-rights request, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, legal obligation.

LUDWISE keeps a message while the matter is open.
LUDWISE deletes it 12 months after the last message unless a legal duty requires longer retention.
A contact address is not used for another purpose.
LUDWISE has no mailing list and sends no marketing mail.

## Game data and media

LUDWISE gets game information from IGDB and store information from Steam.
This provider information describes games, offers, and stores.
It is not used to build a visitor profile.

Game images are served from the LUDWISE domain.
Your browser does not contact the provider network to load those images.
LUDWISE requests the image from the provider and serves the result to your browser.
The provider sees the LUDWISE request and does not receive your browser request for that image.

LUDWISE stores provider game and offer data in the LUDWISE backend on Cloudflare D1.
The repository does not declare a fixed physical country for that database.
The exact Steam Data storage country must be verified from the production Cloudflare database before launch.

See [Data sources](/legal/sources) for provider attribution and the source of each data type.

## Outbound links

A store offer or video link can take you to another website.
LUDWISE opens these links with referrer suppression and does not append tracking or affiliate parameters at launch.
After you follow a link, the destination receives your request under its own privacy terms.

## Service providers and systems

Cloudflare hosts the public website, runs the LUDWISE Workers, stores operational logs, and provides D1 for provider data.
The LUDWISE backend receives server-to-server requests from the web Worker through a private service binding.
Fastmail processes contact email for LUDWISE.

LUDWISE uses no analytics vendor, error-tracking vendor, advertising vendor, or external font service at launch.

## Your rights

Applicable data-protection law can give you rights over personal data about you.
These rights can include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability where applicable.
You can also complain to a competent data-protection authority.

Send a request to [hello@ludwise.com](mailto:hello@ludwise.com).
LUDWISE can ask for information needed to verify a request before acting on it.

## Changes to this policy

LUDWISE can update this policy when the service or its data practices change.
The page shows its effective date and last update date.
