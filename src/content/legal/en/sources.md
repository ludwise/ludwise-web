## About this page

This page names where the information on LUDWISE comes from, and gives the credit each provider
asks for.

## Game information

Game information on LUDWISE comes from IGDB.com. Every game page lists
the sources for that page.

IGDB is a Twitch service. LUDWISE uses the IGDB API under the Twitch Developer Services Agreement.

## Offers and prices

Store offers and prices come from Steam, a Valve service.

## No affiliation

LUDWISE is not affiliated with IGDB, Twitch or Valve. None of these companies endorses LUDWISE.
